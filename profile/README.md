
<h1 align = "center">Welcome to the NERVLAB!</h1>

You've arrived at the official GitHub page for the **Neurobiology of Endogenous RetroViruses Laboratory**, based at the University of Wollongong.

We are a multidisciplinary team of scientists working at the intersection of **molecular neuroscience**, **cell biology**, and **big data science** to uncover how endogenous retroviruses contribute to human health and neurodegenerative disease.

---
### **Our research focus**

🧬 Endogenous retroviral elements, which make up almost 10% of the human genome  

🧫 Aggregation of RNA-binding proteins like TDP-43  

🔬 Single-molecule and proteomic methods for biomolecular discovery & diagnosis

💻 Bioinformatics and data-driven tools for neuroscience
